    Services

    Everything outside your front door

    Tenants and customers judge a property before they reach the lobby. We take care of every square foot they pass on the way in.

    MOWING

    Mowing that leaves stripes

    Commercial mowers, sharp blades and a set cut height. Clippings are blown clear of walks and entrances before the crew leaves.

    EDGING

    Edges you could rule a line by

    Every walk, curb and bed line is cut clean on each visit. It is the detail people notice without knowing why.

    BEDS & COLOR

    Entries that get noticed

    Weeded beds, fresh mulch and seasonal flowers at signs and front doors, where people look first.

    TREES & PLANTING

    Planting and tree care

    New trees, palms and shrubs installed and mulched, then pruned for shape, sightlines and clearance over walks and drives.

    IRRIGATION

    Water where it belongs

    Start-up, zone-by-zone checks and repairs. Water goes on the turf and stays off the parking lot.

    CLEANUPS

    Tidy between seasons

    Leaves, storm debris and litter cleared from turf, beds and parking islands, so the site looks cared for all year.

    Maintenance calendar

    Twelve months, planned ahead

    Grounds care is a calendar. You see the whole year before the first visit, so nothing on your property or your budget is a surprise. Pick a season.
